Summer Goal: Stay fit, get powerful, and roll into Term 1 ready to go.
How it works
  • Pick 4–6 sessions each week (mix from S1–S5).
  • Any sport counts – run, ride, swim, gym, ergs, stairs… just hit the effort level.
  • Green = chill but steady, Red = spicy, Blue = strength, Gray = bonus.

Your “superpower” is consistency. Even on busy weeks, 3–4 sessions done well beats 0 perfect sessions. 💪

Jump to week
Week 1–2 · Reset Week 3–4 · Engine Room Week 5–6 · Power Up Week 7 · Test Week

📋 Your Weekly “Formula”

  • 2x Session 1: Aerobic Endurance (45–60 min)
  • 1x Session 2: Power/Threshold (Erg or Alternative)
  • 1–2x Session 3: Strength/Core (30–45 min)
  • 1x Session 4: Active Recovery/Mobility
  • 0–1x Session 5: Extra Session (Optional Volume)

🎨 Session Colour Key

Aerobic / Recovery 💚 Green Zone
Power / Threshold 🔥 Red Zone
Strength / Core 🧱 Blue Zone
Extra / Optional ➕ Bonus

📅 7-Week Progressive Training Plan

Week Focus & Theme S1: Aerobic Endurance (2x/wk) S2: Power/Threshold (1x/wk) S3: Strength/Core (1-2x/wk) S4: Active Recovery (1x/wk) S5: Extra Session (0-1x/wk)
1–2 🏁 Reset & Routine Re-Establish Base & Routine. Comm. Theme: "Setting the Pace" 45 min continuous effort (UT2/Conversational pace). Run, bike, or swim. ERG OPTION: 4 x 5 min (3 min rest) @ R24. NO-ERG ALT: 10 x 1 min hard sprint / 1 min rest. NO-GYM: Circuit 1 (Squats, Push-ups, Plank). GYM: Machine technique, light weights. 30 min Mobility Focus: Gentle stretching, Cat-Cow, Downward Dog. Focus on Hip Flexors.

 Add 15 mins to one Aerobic Session OR Social Activity (Walk/Swim).
3–4 🚂 Engine Room Build Volume & Threshold. Comm. Theme: "Engine Room" 50 min continuous effort. Add 3 x 3 min pieces at a slightly higher effort (UT1) within the session. ERG OPTION: 3 x 8 min (4 min rest) @ R22. NO-ERG ALT: Hill Sprints/Stairs: 8 x 45 sec max uphill sprint / walk down recovery. NO-GYM: Circuit 2 (Add Jump Squats/Lunges). GYM: Free Weights: Goblet Squat focus, Dumbbell Row. 30 min Water Recovery: Continuous kicking with a board or water treading. Focus on Core stability. Add 1x Bodyweight/Core Circuit OR 60 min Easy Cycle.
5–6 ⚡ Power Up Peak Aerobic Volume & Power. Comm. Theme: "Closing the Gap" 60 min continuous effort. Use Fartlek (30 sec hard/30 sec easy) for 10 min blocks. ERG OPTION: 4 x 1,000m (4 min rest) @ R24-26. NO-ERG ALT: Cycling Intervals: 6 x 3 min hard cycle / 3 min easy cycle. NO-GYM: Circuit 3 (Increase Time Under Tension/slow negatives). GYM: Power/Full Body: Heavier focus on form/Squat/Deadlift. 30 min Focused Foam Rolling & static stretching (Hips, Hamstrings). Focus on full body relaxation. Add a second Power/Threshold session OR Long 75-min Easy Run/Bike.
7 🚀 Test Week Re-Entry Taper & Test Prep. Comm. Theme: "Ready to Launch" 2 x 20 min easy continuous effort. Feel "light." ERG OPTION: 1 x 1000m Max Test. NO-ERG ALT: Short Flat Sprints: 5 x 200m near max effort / 2 min rest. Rest/Mobility Focus: 30 min gentle stretching. Complete REST. Minimal activity. Complete REST. Focus on sleep and nutrition for return.
👟 Coach Notes
  • Plan designed for consistency over perfection!
  • Always warm up for 5–10 minutes before the “hard” part of any session.
  • If something hurts in a bad way (sharp pain, dizziness, chest pain), stop and tell an adult.
  • Hot days: train earlier/later, drink plenty of water, and be smart with intensity.
  • Use this plan as your guide – it’s okay to swap days around to fit life, work, and family.
