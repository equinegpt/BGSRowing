Summer Training Plan

Seven weeks over the break. Lake Wendouree, season 2025–26.

The goal

Stay fit, build power, and start Term 1 ready to row.

How it works

  • Pick 4–6 sessions each week from the five session types.
  • Any sport counts. Run, ride, swim, gym, erg, stairs. What matters is the effort level.
  • Consistency counts more than perfection. Three or four sessions done well in a busy week beats none.

Each week

2 ×Session 1 · Aerobic endurance (45–60 min)
1 ×Session 2 · Power / threshold (erg or alternative)
1–2 ×Session 3 · Strength / core (30–45 min)
1 ×Session 4 · Active recovery / mobility
0–1 ×Session 5 · Extra session (optional volume)

Session key

  • Aerobic · steady, conversational
  • Power / threshold · hard
  • Strength / core
  • Recovery / mobility · easy
  • Extra · optional

UT2 = easy aerobic pace, able to hold a conversation. UT1 = firmer aerobic pace, short sentences only. R = stroke rate on the erg.

Coach notes

  • The plan is built for consistency, not perfection.
  • Warm up for 5–10 minutes before the hard part of any session.
  • Sharp pain, dizziness or chest pain: stop and tell an adult.
  • Hot days: train early or late, drink plenty of water, and lower the intensity.
  • Swap days around to fit life, work and family. The weekly totals are what matter.
